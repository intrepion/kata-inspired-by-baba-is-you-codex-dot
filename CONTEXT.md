# Domain glossary

- Entity: one object or word at a grid position; several may share a cell.
- Word: a movable noun, IS, AND, or property tile.
- Rule: a horizontal or vertical noun list IS predicate list.
- YOU: a property giving every matching object directional control.
- Turn: one directional input or wait, including rules and interactions.
- Bedrock: immutable board terrain, distinct from rule-controlled WALL objects.
- Replay: a literal authored input sequence that completes a level.
